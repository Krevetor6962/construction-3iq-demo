# Synthetic customer priority policy

## Priority-1: Reproducible order
Confirmed contractual commitments precede strategic commitments, which precede standard commitments. Within the same priority, use the earliest committed delivery date, then commitment ID as the stable tie-breaker. Allocation rank records this ordering. Project milestones explain impact; changing the rule requires a documented exception and human approval.

## Priority-2: Golden project sequence
The school has contractual priority, delivery at as-of date plus 3 days, and insulation installation at plus 4 days. The apartment project has strategic priority, delivery at plus 7 days, and internal fit-out at plus 8 days. The office has standard priority, delivery at plus 14 days, and reopening preparation at plus 15 days.

## Priority-3: Avoid misleading rankings
Do not rank solely by order value, account priority score, campaign engagement, or an informal promise. Those facts can inform a review but cannot silently override confirmed dates and approved allocation rules. No project label implies a safety or engineering conclusion.
