# Synthetic material substitution policy

## Substitution-1: Permission is not qualification
`substitutionAllowed=true` permits a request for human qualification only. It is not engineering approval, evidence of compliance, or a declaration that two products are equivalent. A false flag means no substitute may be offered under the current commitment.

## Substitution-2: Required evidence
The fictional qualification reviewer must document project-specific suitability and required evidence. The supply planner checks the candidate product's separate inventory pool. The account owner obtains customer acceptance and the sales manager approves commercial changes before an offer becomes a commitment.

## Substitution-3: Controlled fixture
The interior-board pool has 500 / 400 / 300 m2 demand and 750 m2 free stock, yielding proposals of 500 / 250 / 0 m2. The second and third commitments permit review of `PROD-CON-06`; their qualification status is `human_review_required`. The first does not permit substitution. No substitute quantity is allocated in this fixture. Candidate stock also serves other customers and must never be allocated twice.

## Substitution-4: Safe wording
Use "candidate for documented human qualification", not "approved equivalent", "compliant", or "safe replacement". If evidence is missing, state the gap and assign an owner; do not invent an engineering claim.
